How it works

Add photos and notes: the condition of each room at move-in, a rent payment, a repair request.

2

Both confirm

The other person looks at it and signs. Until both of you have signed, nothing is locked in.

3

Locked and exportable

Verify the record whenever you like, and export a dispute packet if you ever need one.

Simple to use, because there is no blockchain

Many digital record systems ask you to set up a crypto wallet, pay transaction fees and wait for confirmations. This one does none of that. You sign up with a username and password, add an entry, and the other person signs it. Your browser handles the digital signing in the background, using standard, well-established signature technology.

No wallet

Nothing to buy, install or keep track of beyond your login.

No fees

There are no transaction fees, and entries are saved straight away.

No public network

Only the two of you can see a lease and its photos. Nothing is published.

Who it's for

Tenants

Prove the condition of the property and that rent was paid, without chasing screenshots.

Mediators and courts

Check the record yourself from the exported packet. You don't have to trust this app to do it.

Security, in plain language

Your key stays on your device

When you sign up, your browser creates a private key and keeps it. We only receive the matching public key, which can check a signature but can't make one.

The server can't forge your signature

Without your private key, nobody, including whoever runs this site, can sign for you or add an entry that looks like you agreed to it.

Anyone can verify the ledger

Your browser re-checks every signature and photo on its own. The exported packet explains how to do the same check outside the app.

Questions

Is this legally valid?

We can't promise how a court or rent authority will treat it, and this project makes no legal claim. What it does give you is a record that both of you signed and that anyone can check for changes. How much weight that carries is for the court to decide.

What if my landlord won't confirm?

Nothing is locked until both of you sign. An event that hasn't been confirmed stays visible to you as waiting, and it is not part of the signed record.

What if I lose my device?

Use "Back up key" in your account menu and keep the file somewhere safe. On a new device, restore that file to keep signing. Without a backup you can still read and verify the record, but you can't sign new entries with that account.

Can events be edited?

No. After both of you sign, an event can't be changed; any edit breaks the record and shows up when it is verified. To correct something, add a new event.
